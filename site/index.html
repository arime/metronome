Common questions

How do I make the click louder or quieter?

Use your phone's volume buttons or the volume slider in Control Center while the metronome is playing. The app has no volume control of its own.

Does it play when the phone is on silent?

Yes. The click plays even when the ring/silent switch is set to silent, because a metronome that goes quiet would be no use.

Why does it stop when I lock the screen or switch apps?

That is how this version works: playback stops when the app leaves the screen, when the phone is locked, during a phone call, or when headphones are unplugged. Press Start again to carry on. The screen stays awake while the metronome plays.

How do songs and setlists work?

Set the tempo, time signature and accents, then tap the title at the top of the screen to open the library and save them as a song. A setlist is an ordered selection of songs, for example one per gig. When you load a song from a setlist, the ‹ and › buttons beside Start step through the songs.

How do I sync my songs between devices?

Open Settings (the gear at the top right) and switch on Sync songs and setlists on each device. They must be signed in to the same iCloud account. Changes usually arrive within a minute. Click sound, haptics and the current settings stay separate on each device.

Sync says "Not signed in to iCloud"

Sign in to iCloud in the iPhone Settings app. Until then your songs and setlists stay on this iPhone, and they sync once you are signed in.

Sync says "iCloud storage is full"

The app's share of iCloud has room for about a thousand songs and setlists in total. Delete ones you no longer need to make room. Until then, some changes stay on this iPhone.

Sync says "Update the app to keep syncing"

Another of your devices has a newer version of the app. Update this one from the App Store and syncing carries on.

How do I turn off the vibration?

Open Settings in the app and switch off Haptic feedback. This turns off every haptic in the app.
