Snappy Metronome

A precise, no-fuss metronome for iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

Precise

Clicks run on the phone's audio clock, so the beat stays steady from 30 to 300 BPM.

Quick to set

Turn the dial, tap the tempo in, or type it. Eight time signatures, and any beats accented.

Feel the beat

A haptic tap on every beat, stronger on accents. Mute the click and keep the light and the taps.

Songs and setlists

Save each song's tempo, time signature and accents, and step through the gig from beside the Start button.

Heard over the band

Seven click sounds, made loud enough to hold their own against music. It plays on silent, too.

Private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Optional iCloud sync keeps your songs on all your devices.
