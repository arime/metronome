Privacy policy

Snappy Metronome · effective 11 November 2026

Support Privacy policy

In short: Snappy Metronome does not collect any data about you. Nothing you do in the app is sent to me or to anyone else.

This policy covers the Snappy Metronome app for iPhone, made by Ari Metsähalme ("I", "me").

What the app stores

The app keeps your settings, presets and setlists on your iPhone so they are there the next time you open it. They stay on the device and are removed when you delete the app.

iCloud sync

If you switch on Sync presets and setlists, the app stores your presets and setlists in your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your other devices can read them. Sync is off until you switch it on.

  • This data goes to Apple's iCloud under your account, not to me. I have no way to see it.
  • Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy.
  • To tell iCloud accounts apart, the app reads the anonymous identifier iOS gives it for the signed-in account. It is kept on the device and never sent anywhere.
  • When you switch sync off on your last device, the app removes its data from iCloud the next time that device is online. To have it removed in any other case, email me and I will tell you how.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login.
  • No analytics, crash reporting by the app itself, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No access to the microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to me. That is Apple's feature and under your control.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

If you contact me

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply to you. If you open an issue on GitHub, it is public and covered by GitHub's own privacy terms.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact
